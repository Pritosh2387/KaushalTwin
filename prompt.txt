# 1. UNDERSTAND THE PROJECT

KaushalTwin helps assess workers who have acquired skills through work experience, informal learning, and on-the-job training.

The platform should help assessors understand:

- What skills a worker claims to possess.
- Which National Occupational Standards (NOS) and competencies those skills may match.
- What evidence supports those claims.
- Which competencies have sufficient evidence.
- Which competencies still need evidence.
- What assessment task should be performed next.
- Whether the available evidence supports an AI recommendation.
- Why the AI generated that recommendation.

**Fundamental rule: AI recommends; the human assessor makes the final decision.**

The application must never autonomously certify a worker.
